The Author

Saranga Thenuwara

AI Infrastructure Engineer  ·  UAE

Saranga is an engineering director specializing in distributed LLM inference, GPU cluster optimization, and production-grade AI infrastructure. With experience spanning large-scale model deployment across vLLM, SGLang, and custom inference stacks, he writes to bridge the gap between academic AI research and the brutal realities of running LLMs at scale.

His work focuses on making high-performance AI infrastructure more accessible to engineering teams without the resources of hyperscale labs — practical patterns, real incident postmortems, and field-tested optimization techniques.
